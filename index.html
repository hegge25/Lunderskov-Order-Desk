About the data All the information available in the case and our report is used. Where they are silent, the gaps are filled with assumptions, only to show how the configurator works.

From the case and the report

  • The PVM: every field, option and cardinality (case, Appendix 1)
  • The AS-IS process: the 17 steps, their owners and lead times (case pp. 6–7, Appendix 3)
  • The TO-BE process: quotation scenario 2, CTO steps C1–C6 and ETO steps E1–E8 (report)
  • The requirements R1–R7 and the lead-time targets (report §3.2)
  • Which PVM attributes each step uses, and the split into CTO and ETO orders (report)

Assumed, to make it work

  • All prices: plates, filters, accessories and engineering hours
  • The locations and their regulations: wind zones, height limits, emission factors
  • The numbers inside the design rules: limits, factors, material densities
  • The TO-BE step durations, sized to meet R1 and R2, and 8 days for the customer's decision
  • The customers and orders on the board
